Full symphony orchestra, triumphant through-composed piece in C major, 104 BPM, 4/4. Instruments: strings (violin, viola, cello, double bass); woodwinds (flute, oboe, clarinet, bassoon); brass (french horn, trumpet, trombone, tuba); percussion (timpani, bass drum, cymbals); keyboards (organ). Melody on violin, bass line on double bass. Structure: Opening pp, Development mp, Climax mf, Close ff.
